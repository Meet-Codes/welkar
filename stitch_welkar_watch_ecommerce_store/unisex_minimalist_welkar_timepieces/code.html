Universal Minimalist Standards

Everything you need to know about fit, strap adjustments, and our Indian atelier warranty.

How does a 38mm-40mm case wear across different wrist sizes? expand_more

Case diameters between 38mm and 40mm are universally recognized as the optimal horological sweet spot. Paired with our curved lug terminals and ultra-thin 7mm profile, the watch case sits flat without lug-overhang on smaller wrists, while retaining crisp presence on broader wrists.

Are extra tools required to swap the quick-release bands? expand_more

Zero tools are required. Every Welkar Unisex band is fitted with an integrated spring-loaded slide lever on the underside. Simply push the lever inward with your fingernail to detach or attach in under 10 seconds.

What does the 2-Year Welkar Atelier Warranty cover? expand_more

Our comprehensive 2-Year Movement Guarantee covers internal quartz caliber precision, hand alignment, and manufacturer defects. Each timepiece arrives with an authenticated serial card registered upon delivery.
