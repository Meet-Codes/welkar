## Brand & Style

This design system transforms an everyday retail destination into a curated, accessible luxury timepiece atelier. It blends modern editorial elegance with performance-focused e-commerce patterns.

### Aesthetic Persona & Direction
- **Sophisticated Horology:** Inspired by classic watchmaking precision, stainless steel bezels, and deep sunburst dials.
- **Editorial Poise:** Anchored by crisp serif display hierarchy balanced against sharp, neutral sans-serif typography for effortless discovery.
- **Tactile Commerce:** Clean card architecture with micro-interactions, distinct collection categorization, and punchy branded crimson accents derived directly from the heritage insignia script.
- **Audience:** Value-conscious horology enthusiasts, everyday professionals, and gift shoppers seeking elevated craftsmanship across Men's, Women's, Kids', and Unisex collections without generic mass-market clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances horological depths with energetic conversion triggers:

- **Primary (`#0B132B`):** Deep Obsidian Navy. Represents premium dials and night skies; serves as the baseline for high-impact navigation bars, primary CTAs, active states, and dark section backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#1C2541`):** Subdued Midnight Slate. Provides supporting structure for secondary interaction states, borders, and contextual banners.
- **Tertiary / Brand Accent (`#E63946`):** Precision Crimson. Directly echoes the brand's script mark, deployed strategically for sale badges, notification pips, price highlights, and conversion triggers.
- **Neutral Base (`#0F172A`):** Deep Charcoal. Provides maximum typographic readability without harsh pure-black visual weight.
- **Surfaces & Tones:** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards against an off-white dial canvas (`#F8FAFC`), trimmed with polished stainless accents (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The type system pairs **Noto Serif** with **Hanken Grotesk** to combine editorial storytelling with commercial clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys the heritage, craftsmanship, and timeless nature of watches. Used for hero titles, collection banners, product titles in detailed view, and editorial lookbooks.
- **Body & Controls (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Clean, geometric, and ultra-readable on all screen densities. Used for product grid titles, specifications (case size, water resistance, movement), pricing, filters, and checkout workflows.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Currency signs and product pricing always use `Hanken Grotesk` medium or semibold to ensure immediate scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid manages product density while preserving spacious luxury presentation:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 3rem page margins. Standard product catalog displays in 4-column cards, shifting to 3-column on mid-desktop for enhanced imagery focus.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins. Catalog collapses to a balanced 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1rem margins. Collections adapt to a 2-column compact grid or full-bleed horizontal carousels with peek indicators.
- **Rhythm Scale:** Spacing follows a 4px/8px incremental cadence. Tighter gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) structure metadata and badge alignment, while `space-lg` and `space-xl` give watches breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages polished micro-shadows and subtle architectural borders rather than heavy blur artifacts:

- **Level 0 (Surface Base):** Pure slate-tinted canvas `#F8FAFC` without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Quick-Shop Overlays):** Elevation increases subtly upon interaction: `0 8px 24px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.08)`, lifting the watch image and displaying inline controls.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Drawers & Modals):** High-level containment with backdrop filtering (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) and deep structural depth: `0 16px 36px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric silhouette. In luxury watchmaking, precision cases, bevels, and clean chamfers dominate over bubbly contours.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to badges, form inputs, buttons, and specification tags.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to product cards, modal surfaces, and dropdown panels.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Used strictly for interactive collection category pills (e.g., "Men's", "Women's", "Automatic", "Chronograph") to invite tactile touch toggling.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick Buy
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Obsidian Navy (`#0B132B`) with crisp white label in `Hanken Grotesk`, 4px border radius. Hover shifts to `#1C2541` with a subtle 1px translate lift.
- **Accent CTA (Instant Buy / Special Offer):** Branded Crimson (`#E63946`) with hover state deepening to `#C92A37`.
- **Secondary / Quick View:** Transparent surface with 1px border in `#0B132B`, smooth fill inversion on hover.
- **Quick-Add Slide Up:** In product cards, appears cleanly from the bottom edge on desktop hover and remains a compact permanent icon button on touch devices.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 1:1 or 4:5 image ratio container with neutral studio background (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Header Badges:** Top left anchor for badges ("Best Seller", "New Arrival", "Save 30%"), top right for Wishlist heart icon.
- **Details Section:** Category caption in `label-sm` (uppercase, tracking 0.08em), watch model title in `title-md`, followed by current price in bold navy and comparative strike-through in muted slate (`#94A3B8`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Discount & Sale:** Crimson text (`#E63946`) on a delicate tinted wash (`rgba(230, 57, 70, 0.08)`).
- **Movement / Feature Chips:** Stainless gray background (`#F1F5F9`) with slate-steel typography (`#475569`), 4px border radius.

### Category Navigation Tabs
- Segmented pill row pinned below the hero: Men's, Women's, Kids', Unisex, and New Releases.
- Inactive tabs feature a subtle border and transparent surface; active tab fills with solid Midnight Navy and white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard text fields and filter dropdowns feature 1px solid `#CBD5E1` borders, 4px corner radius, and an Obsidian Navy focus ring (`#0B132B` at 2px offset).
- Case size and strap color pickers use circular swatch radio buttons with a dual ring on selection.

### Cart Drawer & Micro-Interactions
- Slide-over mini-cart featuring tiered free-shipping progress indicator using Crimson Accent fill.
- Add-to-cart triggers micro-haptic state confirmation, briefly swapping button text to an SVG checkmark before restoring default state.
